Refund Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2024

We want you to be completely satisfied with your Jozini Move experience.
1. Order Cancellations
Before Preparation

If you cancel your order before the shop begins preparation:

  • You will receive a full refund including delivery and service fees
  • Refund processed within 5-7 business days
After Preparation Started

If the shop has already started preparing your order:

  • Refund may not be possible
  • Contact support for review
  • Partial refund may be offered at shop's discretion
2. Delivery Issues
Late Delivery
  • Delivery more than 30 minutes late: 50% refund of delivery fee
  • Delivery more than 60 minutes late: Full refund of delivery fee
Wrong Address
  • If you provided wrong address: No refund for delivery fee
  • If driver went to wrong address: Full refund of delivery fee
No One Available to Receive
  • First attempt: Driver will wait 10 minutes
  • If still unavailable: Order returned to shop
  • Refund of item cost only (delivery fee non-refundable)
3. Product Issues
Wrong Items Received
  • Contact shop within 1 hour of delivery
  • Provide photos of incorrect items
  • Full refund for wrong items
  • Shop may arrange correct delivery
Missing Items
  • Report missing items within 1 hour
  • Full refund for missing items
  • Delivery fee remains unchanged
Damaged or Poor Quality Items
  • Provide photos of damaged items
  • Shop will review and determine refund amount
  • Partial or full refund based on severity
4. Payment Issues
Duplicate Charges

If you were charged multiple times for the same order:

  • Contact support immediately
  • Duplicate charges will be refunded within 3-5 business days
Incorrect Amount Charged
  • Compare your receipt with order total
  • Contact support with screenshots
  • Difference will be refunded within 5-7 business days
5. Refund Process
How to Request a Refund
  1. Go to "My Orders" in your account
  2. Select the order you need refunded
  3. Click "Request Refund" button
  4. Select reason and provide details/photos
  5. Submit request
Refund Timeline
  • Request review: 1-2 business days
  • Approval notification: Via email/SMS
  • Refund processing: 5-7 business days
  • Time to appear in account: Varies by bank (3-10 days)
Refund Methods
  • Cash on Delivery: Refund via EFT to your bank account
  • Card Payment: Refund to original card
  • Mobile Money: Refund to your mobile money account
6. Non-Refundable Items

The following are generally not refundable:

  • Service fee (R5 per order) - unless order completely cancelled
  • Delivery fee - if delivery was completed successfully
  • Digital items or gift cards
  • Items consumed or partially consumed
7. Dispute Resolution

If you disagree with a refund decision:

  1. Contact our support team to review the case
  2. Provide additional evidence if available
  3. Request escalation to a supervisor
  4. If still unresolved, contact your payment provider
8. Contact Information

For refund-related questions:

  • refunds@jozinimove.co.za
  • +27 71 234 5678
  • +27 71 234 5678
Please note: Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of order delivery.
