Getting Started

Creating an account is easy! Follow these steps:

  1. Click on the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner
  2. Fill in your personal information (name, phone, email, address)
  3. Choose your account type (Customer, Driver, or Shop Owner)
  4. Create a secure password
  5. Click "Register" and verify your email/phone

Once verified, you can start using Jozini Move immediately!

  • Customer: Order food and items from local shops
  • Driver: Earn money by delivering orders to customers
  • Shop Owner: Register your shop and start selling online

Yes! Creating an account and browsing shops is completely free. You only pay for the orders you place, which includes:

  • Item costs (set by shops)
  • Delivery fee (based on your location)
  • Service fee (R5 per order)

Placing Orders

  1. Browse shops and select items you want
  2. Add items to your cart
  3. Review your cart and proceed to checkout
  4. Enter your delivery address and choose payment method
  5. Confirm your order
  6. Track your order in real-time!

You can cancel your order if it's still in "Pending" or "Confirmed" status. To cancel:

  1. Go to "My Orders" in your profile
  2. Find the order you want to cancel
  3. Click the "Cancel Order" button

Once the order is being prepared or out for delivery, cancellation is not possible.

You can track your order in two ways:

  • Go to "My Orders" and click "Track Order"
  • Use the tracking link sent to your email/SMS

The tracking page shows real-time status updates and estimated delivery time.

Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods:

  • Cash on Delivery (COD) - Pay when your order arrives
  • Credit/Debit Card - Visa, Mastercard, American Express
  • Mobile Money - MTN Money, Airtel Money, Orange Money

Absolutely! We use industry-standard encryption to protect your payment information. All transactions are processed through secure payment gateways. We never store your full credit card details on our servers.

Delivery Information

Delivery times vary by zone:

  • Jozini Town Centre: 25-30 minutes
  • Jozini Market: 20-25 minutes
  • Lake Jozini: 35-40 minutes
  • Other zones: 35-50 minutes

Delivery fees vary by zone:

  • Jozini Town Centre: R15
  • Jozini Market: R15
  • Lake Jozini: R25
  • eMpangeni: R30
  • Pongola: R35

Cancellations & Returns

Returns are handled directly with the shop. If you receive incorrect or damaged items, please:

  1. Contact the shop through our platform
  2. Provide photos of the issue
  3. The shop will arrange for replacement or refund

Refunds are processed based on the issue:

  • Cancelled orders: Full refund within 5-7 business days
  • Damaged items: Partial or full refund based on the issue
  • Late delivery: Partial refund of delivery fee

Contact our support team for refund requests.
