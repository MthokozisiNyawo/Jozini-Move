About Jozini Move

Connecting local businesses with customers through fast, reliable delivery services.

Our Story

Jozini Move was founded with a simple mission: to make local delivery fast, affordable, and reliable for everyone in Jozini and surrounding areas. We saw a need for a platform that connects local shops with customers who want the convenience of home delivery.

Starting with just a few shops in Jozini Town Centre, we've grown to serve thousands of customers across multiple delivery zones. Our platform empowers local businesses to reach more customers while providing drivers with flexible earning opportunities.

Our Mission

To revolutionize local commerce in Jozini by providing a seamless delivery platform that benefits everyone - customers get convenience, shops get more sales, and drivers get reliable income.

We're committed to supporting local businesses and creating economic opportunities in our community. Every delivery helps strengthen our local economy and brings us closer together.
